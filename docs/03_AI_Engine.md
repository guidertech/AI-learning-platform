# AI Learning Engine

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

The AI Learning Engine is the core intelligence of the platform.

Its responsibility is not only to answer questions but also to behave like a real teacher by deciding:

- What to teach
- When to ask questions
- When to revise
- When to generate homework
- When to unlock the next chapter

The AI continuously personalizes learning for every student.

---

# AI Responsibilities

The AI will:

- Teach concepts
- Ask adaptive questions
- Evaluate answers
- Detect weak concepts
- Find the root cause of mistakes
- Create recovery sessions
- Verify concept mastery
- Generate homework
- Generate parent reports
- Recommend the next learning path

# AI Teaching Process

For every concept the AI follows the same process.

Step 1

Explain the concept.

↓

Step 2

Show a real-life example.

↓

Step 3

Show an illustration.

↓

Step 4

Ask the student if they understood.

↓

Step 5

Generate 2–3 practice questions.

↓

Step 6

Evaluate answers.

↓

Step 7

Continue or Explain Again.

# AI Recovery

The AI NEVER repeats the full chapter.

Instead

It selects only weak concepts.

Recovery includes

- Simpler Explanation
- Different Example
- Visual Learning
- Guided Practice
- Adaptive Questions

---

# Personalization Rules

Every student has a unique learning path.

The AI adapts based on

- Previous Scores
- Weak Concepts
- Learning Speed
- Practice History
- Time Taken
- Confidence Level

No two students should necessarily receive the same explanation, practice set, or homework.

# Final Rule

The AI does not teach Chapters.

The AI teaches Concepts.

The AI does not measure Marks.

The AI measures Understanding.

The AI does not replace Teachers.

The AI assists Teachers and personalizes learning for every student.
